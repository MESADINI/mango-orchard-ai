## Source

- **Citation:** Ahmed, S.I. et al. (2023). *MangoLeafBD: A comprehensive image dataset to classify diseased and
  healthy mango leaves.* Data in Brief 47, 108941. Data: Mendeley doi:10.17632/hxsnvwty3r.1.
- **License:** CC BY-NC 3.0, which allows non-commercial use with attribution. A commercial deployment of models trained on it
  needs the authors' permission.
- **Obtained from:** the Hugging Face mirror `Project-AgML/MangoLeafBD_disease_classification`, pinned to a revision with
  a verified SHA-256. Every image was matched byte for byte to the SHA-256 list published on the official Mendeley
  record. Class labels were checked by confirming that each Mendeley folder maps to exactly one class
  (`ml/datasets/download_mangoleafbd.py`, evidence `results/data/mangoleafbd_download.json`).

  white background, at 240×240, 240×320 or 320×240 px (see the size table). The Mendeley record states that *"around 1800 are of distinct leaves, and the rest are prepared by zooming and rotating"*.

**Leaf Spot (dropped).** The original project listed a sixth class, "Leaf Spot". MangoLeafBD has no such class,
and no openly licensed, mango-specific, labelled Leaf Spot image set was found. Training on images from another crop
or with unclear labels would violate the project's evidence rules. The model is therefore 5-class everywhere
(code, UI and docs). Leaf Spot stays in the treatment literature as reference-only text.

## De-duplication and leakage control (`ml/datasets/build_disease_dataset.py`)

1. Exact byte duplicates (same SHA-256) are removed.
2. Near-duplicates are linked across **all 8** source classes in two ways:
   - **pHash** (minimum Hamming distance over the 8 dihedral views) = 0. Larger distances are unsafe here:
     different leaves on the same white background already collide at distance ≥ 4 (checked visually).
   - **Retrieval + geometric verification** for zoomed or cropped copies. Each image's 40 most similar images by
     DINOv2 ViT-S/14 (dihedral max cosine) are candidates. A candidate pair is linked only if one-to-one SIFT
     matches agree on a single similarity transform (rotation + scale + translation, with or without mirroring)
     with **≥ 12 RANSAC inliers**.

   **Why not a cosine threshold.** Contact sheets per cosine band (`results/data/dedup_review/cos_*`) show that
   even at cosine ≥ 0.96 many same-class pairs are *different* leaves. A global embedding encodes "this class of
   leaf on white", not "this physical leaf". Thresholding it would merge most of a class into one giant group.

   **How the parameters were chosen** (`--calibrate`, evidence `results/data/disease_dedup_calibration.json` and
   `results/data/dedup_review/inliers_*`):
   - *Inlier threshold:* no cross-class pair is linked at any tested threshold (0 of 4,196 cross-class candidates
     at k=40, down to 10 inliers). Cross-class pairs cannot be the same leaf, so this bounds the false-link rate.
     The 12–18 inlier band is visually almost all the same leaf.
   - *k:* 40 is converged; going to k=80 changes the group count by under 1%.
   - *Check against the authors:* the number of groups agrees with the authors' "around 1,800 distinct leaves".

   An earlier version without one-to-one matching produced false links: a 1000-keypoint image could "match" a
   20-keypoint image hundreds of times. This was found, fixed and regression-tested
   (`test_geometric_inliers_never_exceed_keypoints_of_either_image`).
3. Images linked by either signal are merged with union-find. Each connected component is a *group*, taken to be one
   physical leaf. A group containing several labels is a label conflict, and all its images are excluded.
4. **Oversized groups** (one leaf copied more often than 5% of its class, e.g. a single Sooty Mould leaf with 189
   copies) always go to train (`cv_fold = -2`, never held out). Placed in val or test, one leaf would dominate that
   class's metrics, and StratifiedGroupKFold cannot balance them.
5. A group-aware, class-stratified split (StratifiedGroupKFold, seed 42) divides the remaining groups 70/15/15 into
   train/val/test. It also assigns 5 group-aware CV folds over train+val (`cv_fold` 0–4; −1 = test, never in CV).

## Known limitations

- Lab-style photos: one leaf on a plain white background. Accuracy on this test split is an upper bound and says
  little about field photos. Use the external set (`docs/datasets/external.md`) and field images
  (`ml/datasets/field/`) for that.
- Single country and season; Indian cultivars and the Krishnagiri/Dharmapuri climate are not represented.
- Grouping is an estimate. Copies of nearly featureless (healthy) leaves give few SIFT keypoints and can escape
  linking, and a few look-alike leaves can be merged. The calibration evidence above bounds both, but
  some residual leakage between splits cannot be ruled out.
- The class balance after grouping is uneven: some classes are mostly copies of few leaves (see the groups per
  class in `results/data/disease_dataset.json`).
- Non-commercial license (see above).
