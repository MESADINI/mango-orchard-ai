## 2. Disease Management Source Directory

### Anthracnose (*Colletotrichum gloeosporioides*)
- **Chemical Control**:
  - *Copper Oxychloride 50% WP*: 3 g/L water (0.3%) applied at blossom emergence.
  - *Carbendazim 50% WP*: 1 g/L water or *Azoxystrobin 23% SC*: 1 mL/L water during flush periods.
- **Organic / Biological Control**:
  - *Trichoderma viride* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* foliar spray @ 5 g/L water.
- **Source**: TNAU Agritech Portal (Crop Protection: Horticulture - Mango Anthracnose); ICAR-CISH Technical Bulletin 2022.

### Bacterial Canker (*Xanthomonas citri* pv. *mangiferaeindicae*)
- **Chemical Control**:
  - *Streptocycline (Streptomycin sulphate 90% + Tetracycline hydrochloride 10%)*: 100 ppm (1 g in 10 L water) mixed with *Copper Oxychloride*: 2.5 g/L water. Spray at 15-day intervals.
- **Cultural Practices**:
  - Pruning affected twigs 5 cm below infected tissue followed by Bordeaux paste (1%) swab.
- **Source**: DPPQS Standard Pest Management Guidelines; ICAR-CISH Advisory Note #12.

### Powdery Mildew (*Oidium mangiferae*)
- **Chemical Control**:
  - *Wettable Sulphur 80% WP*: 2 g/L water at initiation of inflorescence.
  - *Hexaconazole 5% EC*: 1 mL/L water or *Dinocap 48% EC*: 1 mL/L water upon first sighting of white powdery patches.
- **Source**: TNAU Agritech Portal (Horticultural Crops - Diseases of Mango); ICAR-IIHR Bengaluru.

### Sooty Mold (*Capnodium mangiferae*)
- **Management Protocol**:
  - Step 1: Manage honeydew-secreting insect vectors (Scale insects, Mealybugs, Hoppers) using neem oil or approved insecticides.
  - Step 2: Foliar spray of *Starch solution (Maida starch 2%)*: 20 g boiled in 1 L water. As the starch dries on the foliage, it flakes off, dislodging the black fungal crust without damaging leaf epidermis.
- **Source**: TNAU Horticultural College and Research Institute, Periyakulam.

### Leaf Spot (*Pestalotiopsis mangiferae*) — reference only
> Not a model class (no licensed training images) and not in `recommendations_db.json`; kept for reference.

- **Chemical Control**:
  - *Mancozeb 75% WP*: 2.5 g/L water or *Chlorothalonil 75% WP*: 2 g/L water at 14-day intervals.
- **Source**: ICAR-CISH Technical Monograph Series on Mango Foliar Diseases.

## 3. Pest Management Source Directory

### Fruit Fly (*Bactrocera dorsalis*)
- **Etiology & Control**:
  - Methyl Eugenol parapheromone traps @ 10-15 traps/hectare placed 60 days before fruit harvest.
  - Bait spray: *Protein hydrolysate / Jaggery (100 g)* + *Malathion 50% EC (20 mL)* in 10 L water sprayed on lower canopy surfaces.
- **Source**: ICAR-CISH Lucknow & TNAU Department of Agricultural Entomology.

### Mealy Bug (*Drosicha mangiferae*)
- **Etiology & Control**:
  - Trunk banding: Fasten 30 cm wide 400-gauge polyethylene film around the main trunk 50 cm above ground level in December before nymph emergence.
  - Soil treatment around tree basin with *Chlorpyrifos 1.5% DP* dust @ 250 g/tree.
  - Foliar spray of *Buprofezin 25% SC* @ 1.5 mL/L water or *Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm* @ 2 mL/L.
- **Source**: DPPQS Integrated Pest Management Schedule for Mango; TNAU Portal.

### Scale Insect (*Coccus viridis* / *Hemiberlesia lataniae*)
- **Control**:
  - Spray *Dimethoate 30% EC* @ 1.7 mL/L water or *Fish Oil Rosin Soap (FORS)* @ 25 g/L water.
- **Source**: ICAR-IIHR Entomological Bulletin.

### Mango Hopper & Stem Borer (*Unreliable / Gated Classes*)
- **Data Policy**: Marked as `UNRELIABLE_CLASSES` due to sub-threshold training sample size ($<100$ annotated images).
- **ICAR Standard Intervention**:
  - Hopper: *Imidacloprid 17.8% SL* @ 0.3 mL/L water at panicle emergence.
  - Stem Borer: Injection of *Dichlorvos 76% EC* (5 mL) into larval tunnel followed by sealing with wet clay.
- **Source**: ICAR-CISH Technical Advisory; TNAU Pest Repository.
